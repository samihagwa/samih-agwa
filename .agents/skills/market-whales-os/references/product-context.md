# Product context

## Current reality

- Samih Agwa owns and manages Market Whales.
- The team currently coordinates through Telegram, mainly the “required work” topic.
- `@teamwhalesbot` and its existing tasks are a test; the team has not been onboarded to it.
- Content work commonly passes through recording, editing, thumbnail design, captioning, scheduling, publishing, and measurement by different people.
- Deliverables may be stored in Google Drive.
- Leads can come from `samihagwa.com/whales-zone/`, Meta, the public site/application, Telegram, and future sources.
- Samih does not currently control the existing Market Whales application source code. Treat it as an external system.

## Destination

Build an internal, web-first operating system on `samihagwa.com` or an approved subdomain. It should unify:

- team tasks and accountability;
- quarterly plans and content production;
- launches, webinars, courses, and campaigns;
- customers, leads, and cross-channel identity;
- analytics, targets, experiments, and decisions;
- brand knowledge and onboarding;
- Telegram notifications and lightweight actions.

## Product principles

- The web app is the source of truth; Telegram is a notification and action surface.
- Every work item has one accountable owner, a deadline, an optional acceptance definition, and a visible next state.
- Review is chosen per task. Direct-close tasks end with the assignee; reviewed tasks can only be approved or returned by the requester or platform leadership, never by the assignee.
- Every workflow transition has an actor, timestamp, validation, and audit trail.
- Dashboards answer a decision question; they do not display decorative numbers.
- Brand and operating knowledge must be accessible to a new designer, editor, publisher, or manager without oral handover.
